FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Let’s answer a few
questions upfront.

What you need to know about how Scrawl works and how to pick the right edition.

Does Scrawl come with its own built-in AI assistant?

Scrawl’s approach is to connect the AI tools you already use to your workspace over MCP. You choose the model and client; Scrawl holds your documents, tasks, boards, and structured apps.

What does MCP actually give me?

MCP lets compatible AI tools read and update notes, tasks, kanban cards, and collections in Scrawl through structured tool calls—so you can keep moving in the same workspace instead of copy-pasting context into new chats.

Can I connect ChatGPT or Gemini to Scrawl Desktop?

Direct connections from cloud-hosted AI clients require a Scrawl Server reachable over HTTPS. Cloud client support also depends on your specific AI product, plan, and version. With Scrawl Desktop, you can connect local MCP tools running on your machine.

Do automations run while my computer is turned off?

Yes, when Scrawl Server remains reachable and your connected bot runs in a separate always-on cloud or server environment. Scheduling and event triggers are handled by your bot or automation service.

What happens if I switch AI tools later?

All of your workspace content stays in Scrawl. Simply configure MCP access for your new tool and continue working with the exact same notes, boards, and records. Chat history inside your previous AI client is not transferred automatically.
